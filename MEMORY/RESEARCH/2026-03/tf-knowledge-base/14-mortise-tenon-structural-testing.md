## 2. Specific Gravity и Yield Stress Correlation — Проектное уравнение

### 2.1 Корреляционное уравнение (Equation 4-2)

Фундаментальная корреляция Miller & Schmidt для yield stress в peg:

```
Fvy = 4810 × G_PEG^0.926 × G_BASE^0.778
```

Где:
- `Fvy` — shear yield stress в peg (psi)
- `G_PEG` — specific gravity of peg (range: 0.6-0.8)
- `G_BASE` — specific gravity of base timber (range: 0.35-0.75)
- G_PEG **must always** be higher than G_BASE
- R² = 0.803

### 2.3 Проектное уравнение (Design Equation 5-1)

С учётом factor of safety 2.20 и load duration factor 1.6 (10-minute test → 10-year standard):

```
Fv = 1365 × G_PEG^0.926 × G_BASE^0.778
```

Где `Fv` — **allowable** shear stress в peg (psi), для standard 10-year load duration.

### 2.4 Пример проектного расчёта (Table 5-3)

```
Данные:
  G_PEG  = 0.73 (White Oak)
  G_BASE = 0.43 (Yellow Poplar)
  Pegs   = 2 штуки, diameter = 1 inch
  Load Duration = 1.6 (Wind Load per NDS)

Расчёт:
  Fv = 1365 × 0.73^0.926 × 0.43^0.778 = 529 psi
  Shear Area = 2 pegs × 2 shear planes × π/4 × 1² = 3.142 in²
  Joint Capacity = 529 × 3.142 = 1,662 lb (7.4 kN)
  With CD=1.6: Joint Capacity = 1,662 × 1.6 = 2,659 lb (11.8 kN)
```

## 3. Failure Modes (Yield Modes) для Pegged M&T

### 3.1 Наблюдаемые yield modes (Schmidt & Daniels, 1999; Miller 2004)

В отличие от стандартных NDS modes для стальных bolts, деревянные pegs добавляют **Mode V**:

| Mode | Описание | Когда возникает |
|------|----------|----------------|
| **Mode Im** | Crushing/bearing failure в main member (tenon) | Мягкий tenon, жёсткий peg |
| **Mode Is** | Crushing/bearing failure в side member (mortise) | Мягкий mortise |
| **Mode Id** | Peg bearing failure | Предложен Daniels |
| **Mode IIIs** | Peg bending — одна/две flexural hinges + crushing base material | **Наиболее частый** для pegged M&T |
| **Mode Vd** | Double shear failure of peg | Твёрдый base material (oak-oak) |

**Mode II и Mode IIIm** — **NOT applicable** из-за геометрических ограничений double shear connection.
**Mode IV** — **NOT observed** в pegged mortise and tenon connections.

### 3.2 Четыре failure modes при tension testing (Miller 2004)

1. **Mortise splitting** — split от peg hole наружу (tension perp. to grain). Причина: inadequate **edge distance**
2. **Tenon relish failure** — single split или block rupture за peg hole. Причина: inadequate **end distance**
3. **Peg bending** — flexural hinge в центре tenon (**ductile**, most common in soft base material)
4. **Peg shearing** — peg shears at mortise-tenon interface planes (**more common in hard base material**)

**Критический принцип**: Detailing должно обеспечить **peg failure** (ductile) ПРЕЖДЕ member failure (brittle). Pegs — ремонтопригодны, timber — нет.

### 3.3 Minimum Detailing Requirements (Table 2-3)

Минимальные расстояния как множитель diameter D (1" pegs):

| Species | End Distance le/D | Edge Distance lv/D | Spacing ls/D |
|---------|------------------|-------------------|-------------|
| Douglas Fir | 2.0 | 2.5 | 2.5 |
| Eastern White Pine | 4.0 | 4.0 | 3.0 |
| Red & White Oak | 2.0 | 2.0 | 2.5 |
| Southern Yellow Pine | 2.0 | 2.0 | 3.0 |

**Важно**: Drawboring увеличивает stiffness но **НЕ меняет** yield capacity. При drawboring: +0.5D к end distances (Schmidt & Scholl, 2000).

#### 5.1.2 КРИТИЧЕСКИЕ РЕЗУЛЬТАТЫ — 1S1B Frame Stiffness

**Design requirement**: При allowable drift = H/400 для 8-ft frame → allowable drift = 0.24". Design wind load = 930 lb.
**Required stiffness**: 930/0.24 = **3,720 lb/in** (minimum).

**РЕЗУЛЬТАТ: ВСЕ frames НЕ ПРОШЛИ** — stiffness ниже required:

- Douglas Fir: **~980 lb/in** — 26% от required
- Eastern White Pine: **~1,460 lb/in** — 39% от required
- White Oak: **~3,000 lb/in** — 81% от required (closest)

**Knee brace стоп-эксперименты**:
- Без knee braces (E. White Pine): stiffness упала до **300 lb/in** (vs 1,460 with braces)
- Free displacement без braces: 0.5" (vs ~0 with braces + gravity)
- Steel dowels вместо pegs: 1,460 → **1,510 lb/in** (negligible difference!)

**Compression vs tension knee brace**:
- Initial compressive stiffness (peg transfer): **~16,000 lb/in**
- Secondary compressive stiffness (shoulder bearing): **significantly higher**
- Compression knee brace carries **significantly more** force than tension brace

**Cyclic effects** (Port Orford Cedar, 350 cycles):
- Stiffness: 1,410 → **1,640 lb/in** (slight increase, densification)
- Free displacement: 0.25" → **0.75"** (dramatic increase!)

#### 5.1.3 Maximum Load Capacity

При ~3" displacement frames **continued to carry increasing load** — strength is NOT the problem, stiffness IS.


## 6. TFEC Standards — Ключевые положения

### 6.1 TFEC 1-2019 — Standard for Design of Timber Frame Structures

**Scope**: Supplement к NDS (National Design Specification for Wood Construction).

**Peg material requirements** (Section 1.6):
- Oven-dry specific gravity: **min 0.50, max 0.86**
- Wedge specific gravity: min 0.50
- Spline specific gravity: min 0.50

**Key notation**:
- `F_em`, `F_es` — dowel bearing strength (main/side member)
- `F_yb` — dowel bending yield strength
- `D` — peg diameter
- `G_t`, `G_p` — specific gravity (timber/peg)
- `l_m`, `l_s` — bearing lengths
- `l_v`, `l_e` — edge/end distances

**Design approach**: Modified NDS yield model equations с добавлением Mode V для wood pegs.

### 6.2 TFEC 2-2018 — Code of Standard Practice

**Fabrication Tolerances (Section 6.02)** для S4S timber:

| Parameter | Tolerance |
|-----------|-----------|
| Timber length (shoulder to shoulder) | +/- 1/8" |
| Mortise length | -0" to +1/16" |
| Mortise depth | -0" to +1/2" |

## 7. Fatigue и Damage Evolution в M&T Joints

### 7.1 White Oak T-shaped Joints (Scientific Reports, 2024)

**Источник**: "Fatigue and damage evolution in wood T-shaped mortise and tenon joints", PMC, 2024.
- [DOI/PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11401834/)

**Material**: White Oak (Quercus alba), G = 0.68, MC = 9.95%

**Specimen Dimensions**:
- Posts: 200×40×40 mm
- Rails: 180×40×40 mm
- Tenon: 30×35×16 mm
- Fit clearance: 0 mm +/- 0.01

**Static Test Results (Table 2)**:
- **Average max bending moment capacity**: 0.105 kN·m (S.D. = 0.001)
- **Stiffness**: 1.081 kN·m/rad (S.D. = 0.15)
- **Average static failure load**: 950 N
- **Yield rotation**: ~6 deg
- **Max strength rotation**: ~16 deg
- **Final failure rotation**: ~28 deg

**Fatigue Test Results (Table 3)**:

| Stress Level | Max BMC (kN·m) | Max Load (N) | Cycles | Status |
|-------------|---------------|-------------|--------|--------|
| 70% | 0.073 | 665 | 20,000 | NOT broken |

**Test Parameters**: Frequency 0.3 Hz, Load ratio R = 0.1, Sine wave

**Ключевые выводы**:
- Deformation grows nonlinearly with cycle count
- Work hardening process in first few cycles
- Energy loss per cycle: constant at 70-100% levels, decreases then increases at 130%
- Failure mode: deformation/fracture of tenon → dislodgement from mortise

**Источник**: "Theoretical Model of Bending Moment for Straight Mortise-and-Tenon Joints with Wooden Pegs Involving a Gap", 2022.
- [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8911610/)

**Material Properties — Chinese Fir (Table 2)**:

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Compressive strength parallel to grain | 31.13 +/- 0.91 MPa |

| Tensile strength along grain | 75.27 +/- 6.97 MPa |

| Friction coefficient mu | 0.42 |
| Moisture content | 11.3-12.4% |

**Specimen Dimensions**: H=1085 mm, L=850 mm, h=160 mm, D=180 mm

**Gap configurations tested**:

| Specimen | Height (mm) | Width (mm) | Gap-gs (mm) | Gap-gx (mm) |
|----------|------------|-----------|------------|-----------|
| BS1 | 160 | 50 | 2 | 1 |
| BS2 | 160 | 40 | 2 | 2 |
| BS3 | 130 | 50 | 2 | 2 |

**Bending Moment Results (kN·m)**:

| Specimen | Yield Moment (exp) | Yield Moment (calc) | Ultimate Moment (exp) | Ultimate Moment (calc) |
|----------|-------------------|-------------------|---------------------|---------------------|
| BS1 | **1.65** | 1.72 | **3.22** | 2.93 |
| BS2 | **1.02** | 0.92 | **2.77** | 2.36 |
| BS3 | **1.16** | 1.13 | **2.73** | 2.53 |

**Model accuracy**:
- Yield moment: Mcalc/Mexp average = 0.970 (SD = 0.070)
- Ultimate moment: Mcalc/Mexp average = 0.897 (SD = 0.042)

**Key equations (simplified form)**:

Yield stress correlation для M&T joint:
```
Fvy = alpha × G_PEG^beta × G_BASE^gamma
```

Bending moment для elastic compression state:
```
M_Ie = (sigma_I × L_I × b / 2) × (D/2 - L_I/3) + (h/4) × sigma_I × L_I × b × mu
```

**Parameter sensitivity**:
- Beam height: minor effect pre-yield; significant post-yield (stiffness + moment increase)
- Column diameter: initial stiffness + moment increase; free rotation decreases
- Gap size: initial stiffness + moment DECREASE as gap increases
- Friction coefficient: both pre- and post-yield stiffness increase

## 12. Varied-Angle M&T Connections — Miller & Schmidt

**Источник**: "Tensile Strength of Varied-Angle Mortise and Tenon Connections in Timber Frames", Journal of Structural Engineering, Vol 138, No 5, 2012.
- [ASCE](https://ascelibrary.org/doi/abs/10.1061/(ASCE)ST.1943-541X.0000468)

**Test Setup**: 12 full-size connections: 4×90deg, 4×67.5deg, 4×45deg. Monotonic tensile loading.

**Results**:
- **45 deg connections** → HIGHEST capacity
- **90 deg** → second
- **67.5 deg** → lowest
- Decreased angle → equal or increased tensile strength BUT decreased ductility
- Connection stiffness reduces as angle decreases

**Failure modes**: peg bending, peg shear, tenon tear out, mortise splitting.

## 14. Strategic Summary — Second-Order Effects

### 14.1 Фундаментальное противоречие
- Timber frames с knee braces имеют **исключительную прочность** (strength) но **недостаточную жёсткость** (stiffness)
- All unsheathed frames failed stiffness criteria H/400 — нужна **supplementary lateral system** (SIP, shear walls, infill)
- White Oak — единственная порода, приблизившаяся к required stiffness (81%)

### 14.2 Implications для проектирования
1. **НЕ рассчитывать** knee braces как primary lateral system — всегда нужны SIP/shear walls
2. **Compression knee brace** >> tension knee brace — проектировать с учётом shoulder bearing
3. **Peg species** имеет minimal effect на frame stiffness — wood vs steel dowels дают <5% разницы
4. **Joint stiffness = ключевой параметр** для frame modeling (НЕ member stiffness)
5. **White Oak** значительно превосходит все другие породы в lateral resistance (3x vs softwoods)
6. **Drawboring** увеличивает stiffness но не yield capacity
7. **Cyclic loading** не снижает capacity но драматически увеличивает free displacement

### 14.3 Для строительства в России (cold climate)
- **Рекомендуемые породы**: Дуб (White Oak equivalent G=0.68-0.73) для maximum performance
- **При использовании хвойных** (сосна, ель, G=0.35-0.50): усилить lateral system, использовать SIP
- **Peg material**: дуб, бук, клён (G > 0.60) — обязательно
- **Проектное уравнение**: `Fv = 1365 × G_PEG^0.926 × G_BASE^0.778` (psi, 10-year duration)
- **Factor of Safety**: 2.20 (relative to EYM Mode IIIs)
